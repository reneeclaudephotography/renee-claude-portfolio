About Me

Professional, collaborative and committed to continuous improvement.

I am a bilingual federal public servant with over six years of progressive experience at the Department of National Defence, specializing in personnel security, onboarding, program coordination and administrative operations.

Throughout my career, I have progressively taken on new responsibilities, from supporting employee onboarding as an FSWEP student, to coordinating training programs, and now serving as a Unit Security Supervisor. I enjoy creating organized and efficient processes, sharing knowledge and helping colleagues succeed.

My approach to work is grounded in integrity, collaboration, attention to detail and sound judgment. I am always willing to support my team, learn something new and identify practical ways to improve how work gets done.
